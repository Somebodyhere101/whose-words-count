Extraction: 36 fixed neutral sentences unrelated to the benchmark. Three native chat-role wrappers, same surrounding text and token positions. Verify identical content token IDs and positions for assistant, system and user. Means at layer 13: R = assistant - (system+user)/2, U = user - system; record the angle and norms. The system/user direction and its R-orthogonal component are rescaled to R's norm. No benchmark outcomes are used for extraction or layer selection.

Role-routing hypothesis: user placement carried the highest behavioural priority in H/I/M. Move system tokens toward that representation and, in one separate condition, user tokens in the opposite direction. This is an externally assigned source intervention, not a claim that the model has learned normative authority or that all source distinctions share one axis.

Pilot tasks: base tasks 65-66 from the two Control Illusion orderings, 24 conflict prompts. Conditions fixed now: plain, emph, oldR2 (-2R on system only), user2 (+2U on system only), user4 (+4U on system only), orth2 (+2U_orth on system only), pair2 (+2U on system, -2U on user), random2 (same norm and signed masks as pair2, seed 271828). Greedy generation, batch 2, cap 512 new tokens, original whole-response scoring. Also 8 nonconflicting arithmetic/copy/capital utility controls per condition; these are screening guards, not a general utility benchmark.

Screening GO rule fixed now: at least one targeted condition beats BOTH plain and emph by >= 0.10 in mean system-constraint success, with no increase in empty/short/repetitive outputs above +0.05 and at least as many utility-control successes as plain. This is deliberately a coarse pilot gate; a pass is not evidence of generality. Otherwise close this pilot, preserve it as negative, and do not spend cloud credit on a continuation of it.
